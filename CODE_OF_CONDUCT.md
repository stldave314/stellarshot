# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not the person who
wrote it.

## What is expected

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, apologise, and move on.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report problems by opening an issue, or privately through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
if the issue involves a maintainer or is sensitive.

Reports will be reviewed and acted on as promptly as circumstances allow.
Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of anyone who reports an
incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not follow this
document, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
